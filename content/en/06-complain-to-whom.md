# 6. Complain to whom?

> "Complain to a friend and you grieve him, or to an enemy and he gloats." A wise man, quoted by Dr. Saad Al-Arifi ([32:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=1970s)) <!-- F071 F072 -->

## From the episode

- **Two kinds of complaint.** To people, and to God. <!-- F066 -->
- **Complaining to people.** Some tell everyone, in every gathering, even the stranger in a clinic waiting room. He says this does not relieve the pain, it renews it. <!-- F067 --> Each time the wound tries to close, you open it again. <!-- F068 -->
- **Complaining to God.** The prophets complained only to God: Jacob's "I only complain of my grief and sorrow to God", and the Prophet's prayer "O God, to You I complain of my weakness and lack of means". <!-- F069 F070 -->
- **The wise man.** He followed a man who had been complaining to a friend and told him: complain only to God; a friend will be grieved and an enemy will gloat. "I have not seen with this eye for 15 years, and I have told no one." <!-- F071 F072 F073 -->
- **The third kind: venting.** Venting is allowed, but the real question is to whom. <!-- F074 -->
- **Two qualities.** The person must have sound judgment and keep secrets: they will not repeat what you said, and they will give advice that helps you out. <!-- F075 -->
- **Rare.** Out of 50 or 100 friends, perhaps one or two qualify. <!-- F076 -->
