# 1. Life on replay

> "You and I are now sitting here for the second time... the life we are living is a film, and we are watching the rebroadcast." Dr. Saad Al-Arifi ([2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=148s)) <!-- F008 -->

## From the episode

- **Three things to know.** Before talking about how to face a crisis, he says there are three things about crises we must know first. <!-- F005 -->
- **First: it was written.** Every crisis that befalls a person was written before they were created, so that, in the words of the verse, "you do not grieve over what you missed, nor exult over what you were given". <!-- F006 F007 -->
- **The match on replay.** You support a team that lost 2-0 last night. You missed the game, so the next day you watch the recording. Do you expect the score to change while you watch? Life is the same: for God it is the second showing, for us it is the first. <!-- F008 F009 -->
- **Second: no load beyond capacity.** A factory writes the maximum load on a truck; load it beyond that and it fails. God, who made each person, knows what each can carry and does not load them beyond it: "God does not burden a soul beyond its capacity." <!-- F010 F011 F012 -->
- **Third: the good may be inside it.** The hardships people go through may hold good for them. He cites the hadith: "How wonderful is the affair of the believer: God decrees nothing for him but that it is good for him." <!-- F013 F014 -->
- **The liver that healed itself.** A man needed part of a liver from one of his sons. Hours before the operation, the tests showed a heart problem: he needed a catheterisation first, and the transplant was postponed. Father and son left in deep disappointment. A month later his liver was working normally. Blocked heart vessels had been starving it of blood; once they were opened, it recovered and no transplant was needed. <!-- F015 F016 F017 -->
- **"Perhaps you dislike something and it is good for you."** He closes the story with this verse. <!-- F018 -->
