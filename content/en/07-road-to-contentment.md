# 7. The road to contentment

> "If you are patient, God's decree passes and you are rewarded. If you are discontent, God's decree passes and you carry the burden." Umar ibn al-Khattab, quoted by Dr. Saad Al-Arifi ([36:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=2209s)) <!-- F079 F080 -->

## From the episode

- **The good life.** He says "the good life" promised in the Qur'an has been explained as contentment. <!-- F077 --> Contentment brings balance, security and calm, and in his view better health. <!-- F078 -->
- **Only two options.** Umar ibn al-Khattab told an afflicted man that the decree passes either way: with reward if he is patient, with a burden if he is not. Panic does not change the hardship, it adds to it. <!-- F079 F080 -->
- **Discontent lengthens it.** In his view complaint and discontent greatly prolong a hardship. <!-- F081 -->
- **His posting to Tabuk.** After graduating he was posted to Tabuk, about 1,300 km from Riyadh. Offered Bisha or Najran instead, he refused all three. <!-- F082 F083 --> He wrote and signed: "I do not want this job." <!-- F084 -->
- **The car that went back.** He got into his car, reversed, and instead of driving away put it back into drive and returned to the same spot. He still does not know why. <!-- F085 --> He took the job unwillingly and, by his own account, not content at all. <!-- F086 -->
- **All the good was there.** Everything he has today, he says, came through that city. <!-- F087 -->
- **His father's plan.** Worried he would quit, his father told him to find a plot of land in Tabuk and build on it, with his father's money. He found a corner plot for 550,000 riyals, built on it, and later sold it for 650,000. His father asked only for his capital back. Tabuk became "a city of livelihood". <!-- F088 F089 F090 -->
- **Grief can kill.** He says grief is a killer in the literal sense, citing the verse in which God tells the Prophet he might destroy himself with grief. <!-- F091 F092 -->
- **The immune system.** In his description the immune system fights invaders efficiently on one condition, that you are at ease; in grief, panic and discontent it shuts down. <!-- F093 F094 -->
- **Contentment is not numbness.** When his son Ibrahim died, the Prophet said "the eye weeps and the heart grieves", and was still content. <!-- F095 -->
