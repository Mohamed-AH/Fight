# 10. Standing by someone in a crisis

> "You made the decision you believed was right at that stage." A cousin's words, quoted by Dr. Saad Al-Arifi ([1:05:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=3908s)) <!-- F123 -->

## From the episode

- **Your role.** Part of your duty to your friends is to restore their balance when a crisis hits. <!-- F120 -->
- **Look at them.** Keep eye contact. Don't look around, don't pull out your phone while he is burning in front of you. <!-- F121 -->
- **Don't criticise.** "It's your own fault" does not restore balance, it destroys it further. <!-- F122 -->
- **Judge by yesterday's information.** A man blamed himself for a decision that went wrong. A cousin told him: you are right; you chose what looked best at the time. Like someone who buys a share on strong news and sees it fall a month later. <!-- F123 F124 -->
- **Keep his dignity.** Pass on advice in a way that preserves his dignity. <!-- F125 --> At school a friend "comforted" a classmate who had left two questions blank: "Don't worry, the resit was made for you and your kind." The meaning was kind; the wording was not. <!-- F126 -->
- **The future is good.** Finally, show him that what is coming is good. <!-- F127 -->

## Words that wound in hospitals

[Watch from 1:11:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=4266s)

- **The hidden crown.** Health is described as the hidden crown: only those who lose it know its worth. <!-- F128 -->
- **A flood of stories.** After he posted a clip about harsh words from medical staff, messages poured in. <!-- F129 -->
- **"Who told you he'll wake up?"** A daughter asked a doctor how her father would be when he woke from his stroke. <!-- F130 -->
- **"May the others be blessed."** A mother asked about her sick newborn. The doctor asked whether she had other children: "you have three, may they be blessed, don't count on this one". The child is three years old today. <!-- F131 -->
- **"Graduate from life."** A student revising for her final exams in hospital was told she might "graduate from life". <!-- F132 -->
- **Kind words heal.** He says gentle words genuinely help recovery. <!-- F133 -->
- **Visitors too.** A visitor leaving a sick man told his son: if your father dies, bury him in the village cemetery with his parents. <!-- F134 -->
- **What to say.** "There's nothing wrong with you" hurts a patient, who wants to feel understood. Say "may God heal you" instead. <!-- F135 -->
