# 11. Are we fragile?

> "Acceptance does not mean surrender." Dr. Saad Al-Arifi ([1:15:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=4509s)) <!-- F145 -->

## From the episode

- **A common word.** "Psychological fragility" has become a very common term. <!-- F136 -->
- **Small things.** People once worried over earthquakes, wars and displacement. Now a manager asks for revisions and the employee decides he is a failure about to be fired; <!-- F137 --> a friend does not reply and "he hates me". <!-- F138 -->
- **Be stronger.** In his view only the strong really live; the fragile, squeezed by trivial events, suffer twice. <!-- F139 -->
- **Managing the heart.** Believe God is with you: "you are in Our sight". <!-- F140 --> Be certain that patience will be well rewarded. <!-- F141 --> Expect relief: "with hardship comes ease", the ease walking beside the hardship. <!-- F142 --> Panic, complaint and protest are what delay it. <!-- F143 -->
- **Accept the nature of the world.** "We created man in toil." <!-- F144 -->
- **Acceptance, not surrender.** Accepting does not mean giving up on change. Keep working to change your situation, while believing God will change it. Rejecting the crisis inwardly makes the effort fail. <!-- F145 -->
- **You are not the only one.** "Death in company is a mercy": no one escapes hardship. <!-- F146 -->
- **A gift.** In his view a crisis is a gift from God, rewarded in the hereafter, while blessings are accounted for. <!-- F148 -->
- **Heart and mind.** The host sums it up: managing a crisis is part heart, part mind. The guest agrees, and turns to the practical steps. <!-- F149 -->
