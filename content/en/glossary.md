# Glossary

Terms as used in the episode and on this site, in English and Arabic. The chapter where each term matters most is linked in the last column.

## From the episode

| Term | Arabic | Meaning | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decree (qadar) | القَدَر | What God has written for a person before it happens. The first of the three truths: a crisis was written before you were created. | [1](01-life-on-replay.md) |
| Capacity (wus') | الوُسع | What a person can bear. "God does not burden a soul beyond its capacity." | [1](01-life-on-replay.md) |
| Rebroadcast | إعادة البث | The guest's image for life: for God it is the second showing, for us the first, so the score will not change while we watch. | [1](01-life-on-replay.md) |
| Test (ibtila') | الابتلاء | Hardship understood as a trial built into life on earth, not as a sign of being unloved. | [2](02-reward-or-punishment.md) |
| Patience (sabr) | الصبر | Enduring a hardship without protest, even while it burns inside. The second of the four levels. | [4](04-four-ways.md) |
| Decrease (naqs) | النقص | The first of two laws: every pleasure, trip and evening ends, and even what lasts loses its shine. | [3](03-things-fade-people-clash.md) |
| Conflict (sira') | الصراع | The second law: people clash everywhere, from states to families and marriages. | [3](03-things-fade-people-clash.md) |
| Discontent and panic (sukht, jaza') | السخط والجزع | The lowest level of receiving a crisis: anger at what was decreed, "Why me?" | [4](04-four-ways.md) |
| Contentment (rida) | الرضا | Accepting what God chose out of trust in Him. It lives alongside sadness and rules out protest. | [7](07-road-to-contentment.md) |
| Gratitude (shukr, imtinan) | الشكر / الامتنان | The highest level: seeing a gift even in the hardship, and thanking God daily for what remains. | [9](09-gratitude.md) |
| The hidden good (al-khira) | الخِيرة | The good in what was decreed. It does not walk beside the crisis but comes "hiding behind" it, later. | [5](05-hidden-good.md) |
| Complaint (shakwa) | الشكوى | To people, it renews the pain; to God, it is the complaint of the prophets. | [6](06-complain-to-whom.md) |
| Venting (fadfada) | الفضفضة | Unburdening yourself, allowed with one or two people who have sound judgment and keep secrets. | [6](06-complain-to-whom.md) |
| Reward and burden (ajr, wizr) | الأجر والوِزر | Umar's two options: the decree passes either way, with reward if you are patient, with a burden if not. | [7](07-road-to-contentment.md) |
| Comparison | المقارنة | "The door to misery" when it is unfair: upwards, on one detail, at a low moment. | [8](08-what-blocks-contentment.md) |
| Gifts, not rights (hibat, huquq) | هبات لا حقوق | Blessings are gifts; treating them as rights makes every loss feel like theft. | [8](08-what-blocks-contentment.md) |
| Social disconnection media | وسائل التقاطع الاجتماعي | The guest's name for social media, which can pull people apart while it seems to connect them. | [8](08-what-blocks-contentment.md) |
| Permanence of circumstances (dawam al-hal) | دوام الحال | The false belief that a hard time will last forever. | [8](08-what-blocks-contentment.md) |
| Psychological balance | التوازن النفسي | What a friend can help restore in a crisis, by listening, not criticising and keeping the other's dignity. | [10](10-standing-by-someone.md) |
| Health, the hidden crown | العافية المُلك الخفي | Health is a hidden treasure: only those who lose it know its worth. | [10](10-standing-by-someone.md) |
| Psychological fragility | الهشاشة النفسية | Breaking over small things, often because the interpretation is bigger than the event. | [11](11-are-we-fragile.md) |
| Acceptance, not surrender | التقبّل لا الاستسلام | Accepting a situation inwardly while still working to change it. | [11](11-are-we-fragile.md) |
| Toil (kabad) | الكَبَد | "We created man in toil": hardship is part of human nature. | [11](11-are-we-fragile.md) |
| Life's circles | دوائر الحياة | Work, friends, health, hobbies and free time, kept apart so a crisis in one does not flood the rest. | [12](12-practical-steps.md) |

## From psychology

These terms are not used in the episode. The site uses them in "The idea, explained" to connect the guest's advice with research.

| Term | Arabic | Meaning | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acceptance | التقبّل | Separating what can no longer be changed from what can, and spending effort only on the second. | [1](01-life-on-replay.md), [11](11-are-we-fragile.md) |
| Post-traumatic growth | النمو بعد الصدمة | Positive change some people report after a hard event: closer ties, new priorities, a sense of strength. | [1](01-life-on-replay.md) |
| Resilience | المرونة النفسية | Coping well with adversity. Some adversity seems to build it more than none. | [2](02-reward-or-punishment.md) |
| Hedonic adaptation | التكيّف مع المتعة | Getting used to good things, so their pleasure fades back to your usual level. | [3](03-things-fade-people-clash.md) |
| Perpetual problems | الخلافات الدائمة | Recurring disagreements rooted in temperament, to be managed rather than won. | [3](03-things-fade-people-clash.md) |
| Benefit finding | اكتشاف الفوائد | Noticing real benefits that followed a hardship. | [5](05-hidden-good.md) |
| Co-rumination | الاجترار المشترك | Talking a problem over again and again with someone, without moving towards a solution. | [6](06-complain-to-whom.md) |
| Chronic stress | الضغط المزمن | Long-lasting stress, which wears the immune system down; brief stress does not. | [7](07-road-to-contentment.md) |
| Social comparison | المقارنة الاجتماعية | Judging our own lives by looking at others'. | [8](08-what-blocks-contentment.md) |
| Three good things | ثلاثة أشياء جميلة | Writing down three good things each evening, and why they happened. | [9](09-gratitude.md) |
| Outcome bias | انحياز النتيجة | Judging a decision by how it turned out rather than by what was known when it was made. | [10](10-standing-by-someone.md) |
| Rumination | الاجترار | Dwelling on a problem; it prolongs low mood. | [12](12-practical-steps.md) |
| Mental subtraction | الطرح الذهني | Imagining a good thing gone, which makes you value it more. | [12](12-practical-steps.md) |
| Self-compassion | التعاطف مع الذات | Speaking to yourself with the kindness you would show a friend. | [12](12-practical-steps.md) |
