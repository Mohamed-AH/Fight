# 9. Gratitude: three blessings before sleep

> "It is enough, for gratitude, that you woke up today." Dr. Saad Al-Arifi ([59:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=3570s)) <!-- F113 -->

## From the episode

- **Waking up.** You wake every day, so it feels like nothing. But every day some people do not. <!-- F113 -->
- **The cadet.** A friend's son had just finished military training. In the barracks his colleagues called him to get up for duty, then called again, and dressed. He was healthy, and he did not wake. <!-- F114 -->
- **Fifty things.** He asks the host to list 50 things he enjoys today. A house, not the street. A car that starts. A switch that lights a lamp, a tap that runs, an air conditioner, a bed, a pillow, clothes. Eyes, legs, friends, neighbours, family. "We'd tire before we finished counting." <!-- F115 -->
- **Three blessings a night.** Each night, lying down to sleep, recall three blessings God gave you that day and thank Him for them: no accident today, a task finished, a good evening with a friend. <!-- F116 -->
- **What it does.** It trains your mind to focus on the good. During the day you start noticing things to put on tonight's list. <!-- F117 -->
- **More of what you think about.** "If you are grateful, I will surely increase you." <!-- F118 -->
- **The whole world.** He quotes the hadith: whoever wakes safe in his home, healthy in body, with food for the day, it is as if the whole world were his. <!-- F119 -->
