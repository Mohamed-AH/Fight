# 4. Four ways to receive a crisis

> "People differ greatly. Let me divide them into four levels." Dr. Saad Al-Arifi ([25:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U&t=1546s)) <!-- F047 -->

## From the episode

- **1. Discontent and panic.** Anger at what was decreed: "Why me? Why would God decree this?" <!-- F048 -->
- **2. Patience.** Burning inside, but saying "God help us" and enduring, without protest. <!-- F049 -->
- **3. Contentment.** "Since You chose this for me, my Lord, I accept it", out of trust in God. <!-- F050 -->
- **4. Gratitude.** The highest level, reached through certainty and contentment. <!-- F051 -->
- **Not a sign of sin.** Some believe misfortune falls only on the disobedient. He says no: you can be among the closest to God and still go from one hardship to the next. <!-- F052 -->
- **The Prophet was not spared.** At Uhud his tooth was broken, his uncle Hamza killed and his army defeated. At the Kaaba, while he prayed, Uqba ibn Abi Mu'ayt strangled him until he nearly died. <!-- F053 F054 -->
- **Nor the other prophets.** He mentions Job's long illness, Jacob's long separation from Joseph, and Abraham thrown into the fire. <!-- F055 F056 -->
- **So expect it.** Expect hardships and program yourself for them, so that you pass through them instead of fighting them. <!-- F057 -->
