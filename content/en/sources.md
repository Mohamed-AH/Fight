# Sources and About

## The episode

- *How to Deal with Life's Crises*, Petroly podcast (بودكاست بترولي), Mukhtalif radio. Ahmed Attar in conversation with Dr. Saad Al-Arifi. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSsimWznk3U), 1:46:51.
- The episode is in Saudi Arabic. Quotes on this site are our translations and are lightly condensed. The site follows the episode's own 17 chapter markers, grouped into 12 chapters.

## About this site

An independent study aid made to help people take in the episode slowly, chapter by chapter, in English and Arabic. It is not affiliated with the Petroly podcast, Mukhtalif radio or the guest. Every statement attributed to the episode is checked against a numbered transcript, and each chapter links to the minute it starts in the video.

Nothing here is medical or psychological advice. If a crisis feels too heavy to carry, please talk to someone you trust, a doctor or a mental-health professional.
